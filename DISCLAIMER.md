# Disclaimer

Pressor is a calculation aid. It is not a medical device and it gives no clinical advice.

- It does arithmetic on the numbers you type in. It contains no drug strengths, no dose ranges and no safety limits, and it never tells you a dose is appropriate.
- **Always check the ampoule label.** Enter the strength exactly as it is printed on the ampoule in your hand. A strength the app remembers from last time is only a convenience and must be confirmed against the ampoule every time.
- Verify every result independently and follow your local protocols and your pump's own checks.
